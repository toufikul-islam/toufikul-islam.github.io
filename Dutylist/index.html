About this roster

This is the invigilation duty roster for the this exam, Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Varendra University — built from the official duty sheet for quick, personal lookup.

  • My Duties — type your initials to see every date, room, shift and time assigned to you (including Answer Script Sorting duty on days you're on that list), and download it as a letterhead PDF — grouped under Regular and Retake/Improvement headings — or a calendar file with a 20‑minute reminder on each item (also addable to Google Calendar, one at a time or all at once).
  • Full Roster — search the entire schedule, grouped under and Retake / Improvement Exam headings, rooms sorted low to high; download a formatted letterhead PDF (room, date, time and invigilator only — room numbers only, no seat counts).
  • Answer Script Sorting — the separate daily sorting‑duty sheet for the regular exam period, with its own PDF download.
  • Swap Duty — enter your initials, the date you're busy and want to give away, and the date you'd rather work instead (only your actual free dates are offered, across both Regular and Retake/Improvement); it returns the initials of colleagues who are free on your busy date and already on duty on your wanted date, so a straight swap works both ways — including swapping a Regular duty for a Retake/Improvement one. The coordinator still needs to confirm the change on the official roster.
  • Show: Initials / Full names — toggle at the top switches how names are displayed everywhere on the site.
